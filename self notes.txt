RETELL AI:
Start your local tunnel: 
Run "ngrok http 8000" on your machine. You will get a forwarding URL like https://1234-abcd.ngrok.app.

Go to the Retell AI Dashboard: Log into Retell and navigate to the Agents tab. Click Create Agent.

Select Custom LLM: When asked how you want to build the agent, choose Custom LLM.

Paste your Webhook: In the LLM WebSocket URL field, paste your ngrok URL, change the https to wss (WebSocket Secure), and add our endpoint path. It should look exactly like this:
wss://1234-abcd.ngrok.app/llm-websocket

Call the Bot: Retell will instantly generate a phone number for that Agent. You can also click the "Test Chat" or "Call" button directly in the Retell web dashboard to talk to it through your browser microphone.
